# 🌍 40 Years of Impact: Global Progress in Torture Prevention

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes 40 years (1984–2024) of global progress in torture prevention using data from the Association for the Prevention of Torture (APT).

The dashboard evaluates how countries have adopted and implemented key legal and institutional frameworks aimed at eliminating torture and protecting human rights.

---

## 🔗 Live Dashboard

👉 [View Interactive Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/ranveer.reddy.deshmukh.pingili/viz/APT_Final/Dashboard_40YearsofImpactAPTsRoleinShapingaTorture-FreeWorld)

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Track global adoption of anti-torture frameworks
- Compare **policy adoption vs real-world implementation**
- Identify regional disparities and long-term trends
- Highlight gaps between **legal commitment and operational effectiveness**

---

## 🧠 Key Insights (High-Level)

- **Near-universal adoption** of anti-torture conventions globally (170+ countries)
- Significant gap between **legal adoption and implementation**
- Institutional mechanisms (NPMs, NHRI) lag behind legal frameworks
- Progress accelerated between **1990–2010**, but has slowed in recent years
- Persistent regional disparities in implementation and compliance

# 🧠 Final Conclusion

Despite strong global progress in legal adoption of anti-torture frameworks, there remains a **clear gap between policy and practice**.

- Legal commitments are widespread  
- Institutional implementation is limited  
- Operational effectiveness is even lower  

👉 This highlights a critical global challenge:  
**Moving from legislation to enforcement and accountability**
